# Chibi Comic & Dialogue Narrative Skill Specification

This skill standardizes the visual aesthetic, dialogue scripting structure, vector speech bubble compositing, and video slideshow timing for BuddyCraft social stories.

---

## 1. Visual Art Style Prompt Rules

When calling image generation models (`gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image`), prepend and structure prompts with these style tokens:

```text
A soft 2D chibi cartoon storybook illustration for children.
Hand-drawn pencil/ink lineart with textured pastel watercolor shading.
Muted, harmonious color palette (soft sage greens, dusty teals, warm tan skin tones, muted corals).
Light cream/off-white background texture.
Chibi character proportions (expressive wide eyes, cute rounded features, gentle non-scary expressions).
CLOTHING NAME PRINTS: Print character names clearly across their shirt/top (e.g., 'AARAV' on boy's t-shirt, 'YAMINI' on mom's shirt).
No floating random text or AI gibberish on background walls.
```

---

## 2. Character Dialogue Script Format

Social story scripts generated by the agent must follow a structured panel dialogue schema:

```json
[
  {
    "panel_number": 1,
    "title": "Arriving at the Office",
    "scene_prompt": "Aarav holding Mom Yamini's hand outside the dentist building.",
    "dialogue": [
      {"speaker": "Yamini", "text": "We are here at Dr. Smith's office today!", "type": "speech"},
      {"speaker": "Aarav", "text": "Will it be noisy?", "type": "speech"}
    ],
    "caption": "Aarav and Mom arrive at the dentist."
  }
]
```

---

## 3. Vector Speech Bubble Rendering Rules

When compositing speech bubbles onto panel images using Pillow (`ImageDraw`):

1. **Bubble Geometry**:
   - White fill (`#ffffff`), dark border outline (`#334155`, width `3px`).
   - Rounded rectangle / oval corners (`radius=14px`).
   - Inner padding: `10px` horizontal, `6px` vertical.

2. **Tail Positioning**:
   - Polygon triangle tail pointing from the bottom/side of the bubble towards the speaker's mouth position.

3. **Typography**:
   - Bold sans-serif or comic font (`DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf`).
   - Text color: `#1e293b`.
   - Text wrapping: Maximum ~28 characters per line inside speech bubbles.
   - Bold uppercase for exclamations (`SUPER!`, `I DID IT!`).
   - Ellipsis (`...`) for quiet / thinking moments.

---

## 4. Video Slideshow Timing

For 30-second video generation (`app/video_tools.py`):
- **Intro Title Slide**: 3 seconds (`30 frames` at 10 fps).
- **Panel Slides**: Distributed evenly across remaining 27 seconds.
- **Dialogue Animation**:
  - `0% - 50%` of panel duration: Display Speaker 1 speech bubble.
  - `50% - 100%` of panel duration: Display Speaker 2 speech bubble.
